About Scheme Finder

Scheme Finder is a deterministic welfare and scholarship eligibility engine. Citizens and students enter their attributes once. The Boolean rule engine calculates matches, displays transparent explanations ("Why You Qualify"), and auto-generates document preparation checklists — without any opaque AI hallucinations.
